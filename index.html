My Journey & Philosophy

I believe great infrastructure should be predictable, automated, and resilient. My work centers on designing automated cloud workflows using Terraform (IaC), containerizing applications with Docker, and establishing zero-touch CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins and GitHub Actions.

Currently, as an AWS and Networking Trainer at LIVEWIRE, I train engineering students in cloud architecture (EC2, S3, VPC, IAM) and Cisco CCNA networking (routing, switching, subnetting, VLANs). Teaching foundational concepts daily has strengthened my understanding of system architecture from the ground up.
